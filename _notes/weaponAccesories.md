## Scope Rules

Scopes are used to increase the range of a firearm, allowing the user to shoot more consistently at longer distances.  

- **Activation:** By spending a **bonus action** to line up a shot with your scope, you increase the **normal** and **long ranges** of the weapon by the amount shown in the table below.  
- **Duration:** This effect lasts until the start of your next turn.  

### Range Modifiers by Scope

| Scope | Normal Range Increase | Long Range Increase |
|-------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| x2    | ×1.5                  | ×1.0                |
| x4    | ×2.0                  | ×1.5                |
| x8    | ×3.0                  | ×2.0                |
